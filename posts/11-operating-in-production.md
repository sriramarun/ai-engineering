## What this week covers

Cost control: prompt caching, the `effort` dial, per-user quotas, model routing, degradation modes. Latency: streaming, edge, prefetching. Reliability: retries, fallbacks, circuit breakers. Observability with Langfuse, Sentry, and Vercel Analytics. Safety: prompt injection, output filters, PII. Runbooks an agent can execute. And the ML question — when a small classifier beats a model call, measured rather than assumed.

## What you'll build
