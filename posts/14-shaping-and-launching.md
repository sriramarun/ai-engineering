## What this week covers

Why "the spec is the work" is more true for AI apps. Customer development for AI features. Writing a spec an agent can execute. Deciding when the model is good enough to ship — with a number, not a feeling. Then: getting Scholar in front of real users, instrumenting feedback, and the rituals that keep an AI app current as models change.
